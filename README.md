# Jaid Shaikh Live Caption

Live captions and a timestamped transcript for any video playing in your browser.
Speech recognition (Whisper) runs on your own computer; no audio is uploaded.

**Open it:** https://jaidshaikh2030.github.io/live-captioner/

1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
2. Play your video in another tab.
3. Click **Start**, choose the video's tab, and turn on **Share tab audio**.

The first run downloads the speech model into your browser's cache.
